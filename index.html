● Live Call Coaching

Control the Call

Professional delivery is not about sounding louder. It is about controlling pace, tone, timing and the next question so the customer can follow you with confidence.

01
Pace
Clear enough to follow. Efficient enough to hold attention.
02
Tone
Warm, calm and certain — never rushed or robotic.
03
Pause
Use silence after the key point and after every question.
★
Sound guided — not scripted
Know the purpose of each line, speak naturally, and move directly to the next required question.
Core Delivery Standard
▶
Speaking Pace
Keep the customer with you
Delivery
Too Fast
Sounds rushed and makes the customer suspicious or confused.
Controlled
Calm, clear and confident. The customer can process every point.
Too Slow
Sounds uncertain and gives a busy customer time to disengage.
Target: Speak as if you are explaining something important to a sharp business owner — not reading from a screen.
❚❚
Power of the Pause
Place silence where it creates confidence
Timing
✕ Weak Delivery
“We help businesses access… PAUSE working capital.”

The pause arrives before the important point and makes the line sound uncertain.
✓ Strong Delivery
“We help businesses access working capital. PAUSE Approximately how much annual revenue does the business generate?”
Rule: Finish the key statement, pause one beat, ask the next required question, then wait.
◆
Punch Words
Give the important words more weight — not more volume
Vocal Emphasis
How to deliver them:
Slow down slightly, lower your voice a touch, finish the phrase cleanly and pause for one beat. Heavy and controlled is stronger than loud.
✓
Confidence Checklist
Reset before every call
Mindset
✓
Sit upright
Posture improves breath support and vocal authority.
✓
Smile naturally
A warmer expression produces a warmer tone.
✓
Remove filler words
Use a brief pause instead of “um,” “like” or repeated wording.
✓
Ask — then wait
Do not answer your own question or talk over the customer.
⇄
Micro-Trust Phrases
Use naturally after every few answers
Rapport
01
“Perfect, thank you.”
02
“Got it, that helps.”
03
“Great, I have that noted.”
04
“I appreciate that.”
Keep it natural: One short acknowledgement is enough. Avoid repeating the same phrase after every answer.
● Objection Control Framework

A.S.E.P.

A four-step structure for handling objections without arguing, over-explaining or losing control of the call. Keep each response concise and return to the script.

AAcknowledge
SSoften
EExplain
PPivot
→
Every rebuttal must return to the next script question
The goal is not to win an argument. Resolve the concern briefly, then continue the qualification process.
Keep Forward Motion
A
Acknowledge
Show that you heard the concern
Confirm the customer’s concern without agreeing with a negative assumption or becoming defensive.
“I understand, [Name].”
“Thank you for letting me know.”
“I’m glad you mentioned that.”
S
Soften
Lower resistance before answering
Use one calm sentence that makes the concern feel reasonable. Do not overdo empathy or sound rehearsed.
“That makes sense.”
“That is a fair question.”
“I can see why you would ask.”
E
Explain
Answer only the concern raised
Give the approved explanation in one or two clear sentences. Avoid guarantees, unnecessary details and unsupported claims.
State the relevant fact.
Keep the wording direct.
Stop once the concern is answered.
P
Pivot
Resume the qualification flow
Move immediately to the next required question. Do not ask whether the customer is interested.
“Approximately how much annual revenue does the business generate?”
“May I have your full legal name?”
“Please hold while I connect you with a senior advisor.”
Live Flow Example
One objection handled from start to pivot
Customer: “I am not looking for funding right now.”
A
Acknowledge: “I understand, [Name].”
S
Soften: “That makes sense — you may not be actively applying today.”
E
Explain: “I am only confirming the basic business details to determine whether the business meets the preliminary requirements.”
P
Pivot: “Approximately how much annual revenue does the business generate?” WAIT
Execution Standards
What professional objection handling looks like
✓
Keep it brief
A.S.E.P. should sound like one smooth response, not a speech.
✓
Use the customer’s name naturally
Use it once to personalize the response — not in every sentence.
✓
Pivot with the required question
Return to revenue, legal name, business name or the transfer step.
✕
Do not argue or repeat yourself
If the objection remains unresolved, follow the approved escalation or call-ending process.
✕
Do not ask “Are you interested?”
That creates an unnecessary yes-or-no exit. Continue with the qualification flow instead.
Agent checkpoint: Did I acknowledge the concern, answer only what was asked, and return to the next approved question?
Acknowledge · Soften · Explain · Pivot
